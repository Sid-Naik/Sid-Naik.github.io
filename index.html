01 / 05

LLM issue triage for the Gemini API

Google, as part of a Virtusa vendor team, 2025 to now

Problem
Developer issues about the Gemini API, AI Studio and Antigravity arrive on a public forum and an internal tracker, and each one has to be routed, answered, reproduced or escalated.
Approach
Gemini-based tooling that routes incoming issues, generates channel metadata, drafts replies and writes reproduction steps and root-cause notes for engineering escalations.
Result
Batch triage time down 30%, measured with and without the tools. The team, which grew from 3 to 6 under my lead, triaged 4,000+ issues and escalated 200+ reproduced bugs to Google engineering.
  • Gemini API
  • Python
  • prompt design
  • evaluation against human triage
02 / 05

Shipment transit-time prediction

US home-furnishings retailer, production

Problem
Operations couldn't tell which vendor shipments would arrive late; the client's existing estimate was off by about six days.
Approach
An XGBoost model on two years of transit and loading data, run as a weekly batch pipeline on Kubeflow with monitoring, and a dashboard for the operations team.
Result
Error down to a 24-hour RMSE with 10% MAPE. Operations used the dashboard to flag vendors that were consistently late. I led a team of four.
  • XGBoost
  • GCP
  • Kubeflow Pipelines
  • Docker
  • BigQuery
  • Streamlit on Cloud Run
03 / 05

Market basket analysis at scale

US home-furnishings retailer, production

Problem
Merchandising teams wanted to know which products sell together for any slice of the business — department, region, quarter — not just overall.
Approach
A monthly pipeline that mines association rules across 80,000 prioritised slices and stores them in BigQuery, plus a service that computes new slices on demand and caches them.
Result
Insights over two years of sales, available on demand. Moving the processing from Pandas to Polars turned multi-day runs into hours.
  • mlxtend (H-Mine)
  • Polars
  • Kubeflow
  • BigQuery
  • serverless
04 / 05

Drone-based damage detection

Global records-management company, prototype

Problem
Staff walked warehouse aisles to find damaged storage boxes. Leadership needed to know, before fiscal year-end, whether drones could do it.
Approach
SAM segmentation and rule-based filtering to isolate boxes in drone footage, a fine-tuned BEiT classifier to flag damage, and an API that turns detections into reboxing requests.
Result
0.90 recall on damaged boxes and scanning 30× faster than manual inspection — enough evidence for the investment decision. Precision was the next thing to improve.
  • SAM
  • BEiT
  • PyTorch
  • Hugging Face
  • FastAPI
05 / 05

Equipment recommendations for hospital clients

Global medical-equipment manufacturer

Problem
The sales team needed to know which products to pitch to which hospitals and colleges, including newly launched products with no sales history.
Approach
Implicit-feedback ALS on purchase ratings shaped with domain experts, adjusted with customer and product clusters to reach new products; a separate model scored how likely a pitch was to convert.
Result
MRR of 0.20 for the recommender and about 80% accuracy for the propensity model, both handed to the sales team.
  • Implicit ALS
  • Random Forest
  • Azure Databricks
